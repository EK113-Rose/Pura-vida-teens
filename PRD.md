👤 Person 2:
Good Evening,

I hope my feedback is helpful!

1. What kind of help would you find useful?

There are several areas where I think families could benefit from extra help:

* Household support: Weekly house cleaning is incredibly helpful. I also have someone who comes twice a month to help with kitchen preparation, such as chopping onions, garlic and preparing fruit. Laundry support would also be valuable, including washing, folding and putting away clothes according to the family’s instructions. I know some friends who have cooks, but I personally prefer to do the actual cooking myself.
* Childcare: Having access to trusted childcare is a huge help. Occasional babysitting allows parents to manage commitments, enjoy time together or simply have some time to themselves.
* School holiday and activity support: As school breaks approach, I would find it incredibly helpful to have someone assist with creating, preparing and facilitating meaningful activities for the children. This could include hands-on crafts, educational activities, scavenger hunts, outdoor games and other creative ways to keep children engaged, learning and having fun during their time off school. I think this could be particularly appealing to families looking for enriching alternatives to screen time.
* Home organization: Decluttering and organizing are other areas where an extra set of hands would be wonderful, especially when moving homes.
* Academic support: I used to babysit and tutor children when I was in high school. I think after-school homework assistance and tutoring could be a really useful service. This could be especially valuable for homeschooling families who might appreciate extra support with specific subjects, reading practice, writing, math or keeping children engaged in learning activities.

2. What tasks would you gladly delegate to someone else?

* Regular home cleaning.
* Laundry (washing, folding and putting away) 

* Kitchen preparation and basic food prep.
* Car washing and cleaning.
* Occasional childcare.
* Preparing and facilitating children’s activities.
* Home decluttering and organization.
* Homework assistance and tutoring.

3. How often would you need this kind of help?

* House cleaning: Weekly.
* Kitchen preparation: Weekly or twice monthly.
* Laundry: Weekly.
* Babysitting: 2–3 times per month.
* Children’s activities: More frequently during school holidays.
* Tutoring/homework support: Once or several times per week, depending on the family’s needs.
* Organizing and decluttering: Occasionally or as needed.


4. How comfortable would you feel trusting a teenager aged 13–17 to handle these tasks?

I think this would depend much more on the individual teenager than their age. Their references, values, maturity and personality would all play an important role in determining my comfort level. I would be more comfortable starting with smaller responsibilities and gradually building trust.

5. What qualities would a teenager need to have for you to trust them?

* Attentive and responsible.
* Respectful and considerate.
* Positive values and a good work ethic.
* Reliable and punctual.
* Willingness to learn, take direction and grow.
* Good communication skills.
* Initiative and the ability to follow through on commitments.

6. What skills or experience would you expect a teenager to have?

I would expect some basic skills depending on the task, but I don’t think extensive experience should necessarily be a requirement. Attentiveness, initiative and a genuine willingness to learn are much more important to me. I think this platform could also be a wonderful opportunity for teenagers to develop practical life skills and gain valuable work experience.

7. What tasks would you be willing to pay for, and what payment arrangement would you prefer?

I would be willing to pay for most of the services mentioned above, particularly household cleaning, laundry, kitchen preparation, car cleaning, childcare, children’s activities and academic tutoring.

I think an hourly rate would make sense for ongoing or open-ended tasks like cleaning, childcare and kitchen preparation. For more defined jobs, such as washing a car, gardening or organizing a particular space, a fixed price could work better.

I would also want the payment to reflect the teenager’s experience, the time involved and the level of responsibility required. Having transparent, agreed-upon rates on the platform would make things easier for both families and teenagers.
